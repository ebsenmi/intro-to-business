Part 1: Plan for the people you need

HR planning starts with job analysis and forecasting. Label each item from your files, then decide what the forecast tells you.

Job analysis: a study of the tasks required to do a job well
Job description: lists the tasks and responsibilities of a job
Job specification: the skills, knowledge, and abilities a person needs to fill the job

Part 2: Recruit applicants

Sunrise has openings at several stores. Choose the recruiting term that best matches each situation. When more than one term could apply, choose the most specific one.

Part 3: Select the right candidate

The selection process has five steps: initial screening, employment testing, the selection interview, a background and reference check, and a physical exam. Identify which step each moment belongs to, then pick the correct order.

Part 4: Train, develop, and evaluate

Your new hires and current employees need training. Choose the training or development method each situation describes, then answer the performance appraisal question.

Part 5: Pay and benefits

Payroll sent you questions about how different employees are paid. Choose the type of compensation each one describes.

Part 6: Labor relations and the law

Some Sunrise employees are talking about forming a union, and a few legal questions just landed on your desk. Choose the term, law, or agency that fits each situation.
