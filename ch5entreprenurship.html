Entrepreneurial Risk Assessment
Six traits researchers associate with successful entrepreneurs. Rate yourself honestly — there are no right answers.
Risk Tolerance
I make decisions comfortably without knowing the full outcome. Uncertainty energizes rather than paralyzes me.
Self-Motivation
I do not need a boss, deadline, or grade to push me. I set my own goals and pursue them independently.
Comfort with Failure
When something I try does not work, I treat it as information rather than a personal defeat.
Opportunity Spotting
I regularly notice gaps, inefficiencies, or unmet needs and start thinking about how to solve them.
Bias Toward Action
I would rather try something imperfect now than wait until everything is perfectly planned.
Resilience
When things get hard or go wrong, I find a way to keep going rather than giving up or stepping back.
