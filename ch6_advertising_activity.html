An advertising appeal is the creative angle a brand uses to connect with its audience. For each real campaign description below, select the appeal type being used.
GEICO: A tiny British gecko explains car insurance with dry wit while absurd things happen around him. The brand runs multiple joke formats simultaneously so no single punchline gets old.
ADT Home Security: "A break-in happens every 18 seconds in America." Cut to a startled family. Then: ADT installed, alarm sounds, family safe.
Always #LikeAGirl: The ad asks people to "run like a girl" — adults perform weak caricatures; then young girls are asked and simply run, hard and fast. No product shown until the final five seconds.
Nike x Michael Jordan (1984): A 22-year-old rookie wears a color shoe the NBA tried to ban. Nike said they'd pay his fines. The shoe became a cultural event before a single ad ran.
Headspace: "Over 70 million people in 190 countries use Headspace. Join them." The ad shows diverse users meditating; user count is front and center.
