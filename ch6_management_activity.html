Meet The Cornerstone Hotel

The Cornerstone is a 180-room full-service hotel in downtown Duluth, Minnesota. It has been open for 14 years and employs 95 people across housekeeping, front desk, food & beverage, maintenance, sales, and administration.

The hotel is about to hire a new General Manager — and the ownership group is making big decisions about how to restructure. Work through the 12 scenarios below and apply what you know about management and leadership to help guide their decisions.
